Roommate Agreement: What to Include (Complete Checklist)

The #1 mistake new roommates make: assuming they agree on everything because they get along. A 15-minute conversation producing a one-page agreement prevents months of tension.

Rent and Utilities: Exact Amounts, Not Percentages

The agreement must specify each roommate's exact share of rent in dollars — not percentages. If rent changes or a roommate leaves, "each person pays their share" becomes meaningless. Write: "Roommate A pays $900 toward rent; Roommate B pays $750 toward rent." Also specify who makes the payment to the landlord (usually one person collects from all and makes one payment).

For utilities: assign exact splits or assign bills. "Roommate A pays electric; Roommate B pays internet; all others split equally" is clearer than "we'll figure it out." Specify payment deadline — by the 1st of each month, with 3 days grace, or you cover that roommate's share and they reimburse you within 7 days.

Guests and Overnight Visitors

Guest policies are the most common source of roommate tension. Define: how many consecutive nights a guest can stay (3–5 nights per month is standard), whether significant others are considered guests or semi-permanent residents after a certain threshold (30+ nights/year in some agreements triggers "guest becomes de facto roommate and contributes to expenses"), and whether guests can use all shared spaces.

Also define: quiet hours (typically 10PM–8AM on weeknights, midnight–9AM on weekends), and whether prior notice is required for overnight guests.

Common Areas and Chores

Assign specific cleaning responsibilities or create a rotating schedule. Cover: kitchen (dishes must be cleaned within 24 hours, shared fridge space, who buys common supplies), bathroom (cleaning schedule, counter space allocation), living areas (vacuuming/mopping frequency), and trash (who takes it out and when).

Also address: can shared spaces be used for work/studying (and when), temperature settings (heating and cooling disputes are surprisingly common), and whether shoes-off is required.

Moving Out and Subletting

How much notice must a roommate give before moving out? (60 days is standard to allow time to find a replacement.) Who is responsible for finding a replacement roommate? (Usually the leaving roommate.) Can the leaving roommate sublet their room without approval from remaining roommates? (Usually no.)

Security deposit: if the full deposit is in one person's name, how is it divided if one roommate leaves before the lease ends? Document the condition of each person's room at move-in (photos) so the deposit return process is fair. The landlord will return the deposit to whoever is named on the lease — internal allocation should be written.

FAQ

Is a roommate agreement legally binding?
Yes, if all roommates sign it. It is an enforceable contract between the roommates. However, a roommate agreement does not change anything in the lease — the landlord is still owed rent by whoever signed the lease, regardless of what roommates agreed between themselves. If one roommate doesn't pay their share, the lease-signing roommate is still liable to the landlord for the full amount.
What if a roommate refuses to sign a roommate agreement?
A refusal to sign a basic roommate agreement is a significant red flag. If a potential roommate won't commit in writing to paying their share of rent, they may not be reliable. You can make signing the roommate agreement a condition of moving in — and you should.
Can a roommate agreement include a "house manager" role?
Yes. Many roommate agreements designate one person to manage the landlord relationship (collects rent, submits maintenance requests, is the point of contact). This person is often compensated with a small rent reduction. The role and its responsibilities should be clearly defined in the agreement.
What happens if a roommate doesn't pay their share of rent?
Under the roommate agreement, you can sue in small claims court for the unpaid share plus any costs you incurred covering it. The landlord cares only that full rent is paid — they will not mediate roommate disputes. Document all payment demands in writing (texts, emails) before filing.
