FAQ

Invoice questions answered

What must a professional invoice include?
A legally valid invoice should include: your business name and address, client name and address, unique invoice number, invoice date, payment due date, itemized list of services/products with quantities and rates, subtotal, tax amount (if applicable), and total amount due. Including payment instructions is also recommended.
How do I add sales tax to my invoice?
Enter the tax rate percentage in the Tax Rate field. The calculator will automatically compute the tax amount and add it to the subtotal to show the final total. Whether you need to charge sales tax depends on your state and whether your services are taxable — consult a tax professional for your specific situation.
What is Net 30 on an invoice?
Net 30 means payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date. Net 15 = 15 days, Net 60 = 60 days. “Due on receipt” means payment is expected immediately. For new clients, shorter terms (Net 15 or due on receipt) are generally safer.
Should I keep copies of invoices?
Yes. Keep all invoices for at least 7 years for tax purposes. The IRS can audit returns up to 3 years back (6 years if substantial income was underreported). Having a complete invoice record helps with bookkeeping and resolving payment disputes.
