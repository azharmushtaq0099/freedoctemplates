Michigan Lease Agreement — Key Legal Requirements

Michigan residential leases are governed by state landlord-tenant law. Key rules for Michigan landlords and tenants:

  • Security deposit: 1.5 months rent. Must be returned within 30 days with itemized deductions.
  • Entry notice: Landlords must give No statute (recommend 24 hrs) advance notice for non-emergency entry.

Michigan has the Security Deposit Act requiring landlords to send a damage checklist within 7 days of move-in and itemized deductions within 30 days of move-out.

FAQ

Michigan Lease Agreement questions

What is the security deposit limit in Michigan?
In Michigan, the security deposit limit is: 1.5 months rent. Collecting more than the legal limit can expose landlords to double or triple damages plus attorney fees.
How long does a Michigan landlord have to return a security deposit?
Michigan landlords must return the deposit within 30 days of the tenant vacating, along with an itemized list of any deductions. Failure to comply can forfeit the landlord's right to withhold any portion of the deposit.
How much notice must a Michigan landlord give before entering?
In Michigan, landlords must provide No statute (recommend 24 hrs) before entering a rental unit for non-emergency purposes. Emergency entries (fire, gas leak, flooding) are exempt.
Is there rent control in Michigan?
Michigan does not have statewide rent control. Landlords may raise rent with proper notice at lease renewal.
