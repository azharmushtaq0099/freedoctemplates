Forming an LLC in Michigan

Michigan LLC Operating Agreement

To form an LLC in Michigan, file Articles of Organization with the Secretary of State and pay the $50 filing fee. While Michigan may not legally require an operating agreement, every LLC should have one — it governs member rights, profit distributions, voting, and what happens if a member leaves.

After formation, Michigan LLCs must pay $25 annual statement. Missing this deadline can result in late fees or administrative dissolution of the LLC.

FAQ

Michigan LLC questions

Does Michigan require an LLC operating agreement?
Most states do not legally require an operating agreement, but it is strongly recommended for every LLC. Without one, your LLC is governed by default Michigan LLC statutes — which may not reflect what the members actually want. Banks and investors often require an operating agreement before doing business with an LLC.
How much does it cost to form an LLC in Michigan?
The state filing fee to form an LLC in Michigan is $50. After formation, Michigan LLCs must pay $25 annual statement to maintain good standing. These fees are in addition to any registered agent fees.
Can a single-member LLC use the same operating agreement?
Yes. A single-member LLC operating agreement is slightly simpler — it covers the sole member's rights, the management structure, and how the LLC will be taxed (disregarded entity vs. S-Corp). It is still important to have in writing for liability protection and banking purposes.
Does an LLC operating agreement need to be notarized in Michigan?
No. An LLC operating agreement in Michigan does not need to be notarized or filed with the state. It is an internal document signed by the members. Keep the signed original with your LLC records.
