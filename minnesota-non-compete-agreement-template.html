Non-Compete Agreements in Minnesota

Minnesota restricts or bans most employee non-compete agreements. Using an unenforceable non-compete in Minnesota can expose the employer to legal liability. Consult a Minnesota employment attorney before asking employees to sign any restrictive covenants.

FAQ

Minnesota non-compete questions

Are non-compete agreements enforceable in Minnesota?
Minnesota does not enforce most employee non-compete agreements. The restriction exists to protect employee mobility. Independent contractor non-competes may have different treatment — consult an attorney.
How long can a non-compete last in Minnesota?
Minnesota does not allow most non-compete agreements, so duration is not typically at issue.
Can an employee be fired for violating a non-compete in Minnesota?
In Minnesota, most non-compete agreements are not enforceable, so termination based solely on violating an unenforceable non-compete could itself be wrongful. Consult an employment attorney.
Does a non-compete need to be notarized in Minnesota?
No. A non-compete agreement in Minnesota does not need to be notarized to be enforceable. Both parties must sign the agreement, which should be provided to the employee before or at the time of hire, along with a copy of what they are signing.
