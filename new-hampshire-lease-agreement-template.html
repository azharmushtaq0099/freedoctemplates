New Hampshire Lease Agreement — Key Legal Requirements

New Hampshire residential leases are governed by state landlord-tenant law. Key rules for New Hampshire landlords and tenants:

  • Security deposit: 1 month rent or $100 (whichever is greater). Must be returned within 30 days with itemized deductions.
  • Entry notice: Landlords must give Adequate notice advance notice for non-emergency entry.

FAQ

New Hampshire Lease Agreement questions

What is the security deposit limit in New Hampshire?
In New Hampshire, the security deposit limit is: 1 month rent or $100 (whichever is greater). Collecting more than the legal limit can expose landlords to double or triple damages plus attorney fees.
How long does a New Hampshire landlord have to return a security deposit?
New Hampshire landlords must return the deposit within 30 days of the tenant vacating, along with an itemized list of any deductions. Failure to comply can forfeit the landlord's right to withhold any portion of the deposit.
How much notice must a New Hampshire landlord give before entering?
In New Hampshire, landlords must provide Adequate notice before entering a rental unit for non-emergency purposes. Emergency entries (fire, gas leak, flooding) are exempt.
Is there rent control in New Hampshire?
New Hampshire does not have statewide rent control. Landlords may raise rent with proper notice at lease renewal.
