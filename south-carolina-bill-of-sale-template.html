South Carolina Bill of Sale — Requirements & Process

In South Carolina, a bill of sale documents private sales of vehicles and personal property. Key South Carolina requirements:

  • Notary: Not required.
  • Odometer: Required for vehicles under 10 years.
  • Title transfer fee: $15 at the DMV.

South Carolina has a maximum sales tax of $500 on vehicle sales (6% rate capped). Title transfer within 45 days. SCDMV Form 400 used for Bill of Sale.

FAQ

South Carolina Bill of Sale questions

Does a South Carolina bill of sale need to be notarized?
In South Carolina: Not required. South Carolina has a maximum sales tax of $500 on vehicle sales (6% rate capped). Title transfer within 45 days. SCDMV Form 400 used for Bill of Sale.
How do I transfer a vehicle title in South Carolina?
After completing the bill of sale, both parties sign the title. Take the signed title, bill of sale, and the title fee ($15) to the South Carolina DMV. The buyer typically has 30 days to complete the transfer.
What should a South Carolina bill of sale include?
A valid South Carolina bill of sale should include: seller and buyer full names and addresses, a description of the item sold (vehicle VIN, equipment description, etc.), the sale price and date, the odometer reading for vehicles under 10 years old, and signatures of both parties.
Do I need a bill of sale for a private sale in South Carolina?
Yes. While South Carolina may use the title itself for vehicle transfers, a separate bill of sale protects both parties by documenting the agreed price, condition, and date of sale — important for tax purposes and resolving any later disputes.
