Bill of Sale in Tampa, FL

A bill of sale for a vehicle or personal property in Tampa is governed by Florida law. For vehicle title transfers, take the signed bill of sale and the title to the Hillsborough County DMV or tax collector's office to complete the transfer and pay any applicable sales tax.

Always document private-party sales with a written bill of sale — it protects both the buyer (proof of purchase) and the seller (releases liability for the vehicle after sale).

FAQ

Tampa bill of sale questions

Where do I transfer a vehicle title in Tampa?
To transfer a vehicle title in Tampa, take the signed title and bill of sale to the Hillsborough County DMV office or motor vehicle department. You will need to pay a title transfer fee and any applicable sales tax on the sale price.
Does a bill of sale need to be notarized in Tampa?
Some states require a notarized bill of sale for vehicle title transfers. Check current Florida DMV requirements for your vehicle type. For general personal property (not requiring title transfer), notarization is not legally required but adds credibility.
How long do I have to transfer a vehicle title in Florida?
Most states require vehicle title transfers within 30 days of the sale. Missing this deadline may result in late fees. After the sale, the seller should remove their name from the registration and notify the DMV to avoid liability for the vehicle.
Can I use a bill of sale as proof of ownership?
A bill of sale proves you purchased the item but is not the same as a legal title. For vehicles, boats, and real property, you must also transfer the title document. For general personal property without a title, a signed bill of sale is the primary proof of ownership.
