Why use free legal document templates?

Professional legal help is expensive — attorney fees for a simple LLC operating agreement or contractor agreement can run $500 to $2,000. For straightforward legal documents with well-established forms, a quality template lets you create a legally sound document in minutes at no cost.

All templates on FreeDocTemplates are designed for US use, updated for 2026, and include the clauses that matter most: IP assignment, confidentiality, governing law, and dispute resolution.
